Proposed Design

Three Loops in Series, Economizer in Parallel

  • Three loops in series: chilled water → R-290 → glycol → air
  • The 30% propylene-glycol loop keeps flammable A3 propane out of the data hall
  • A waterside economizer in parallel with the chiller enables compressor-off free cooling
  • Two parallel VFD compressors on one shared refrigerant circuit

Evaporator — Brazed-Plate HX

Evaporator — two-zone model

  • Two zones — boiling (Han) + superheat (Blasius), split by enthalpy; the superheat tail sets the suction state.
  • 150 kW across ΔTlm = 7.6 K needs 8.2 m² at U = 2,400 W/m²K (double-wall derated): ≈84 plates, ~20% margin.
  • R-290 · Ncp = 38 · Gc ≈ 25.3 kg/m²s — inside Han’s 13–34 kg/m²s band.
ΔP — refrigerant side (boiling zone)ΔPfr = f · (Lv Ncp / Dh) · GEq²/ρf
f = Ge₃·ReEqGe₄  ·  GEq = Gc[(1−x) + x(ρf/ρg)½]  ·  Gc = ṁ/(Ncp b Lw)
ΔP — water sideΔP = 2f · (Lv/Dh) · G²/ρ  —  Martin (1996), single-phase5.973 kg/s · G = 545 kg/m²s · Re = 2,072 · f = 0.109 → ΔP = 9.75 kPa
Where f comes from — Han (2003), evaporation setGe₃ = 64,710·(pco/Dh)−5.27·(π/2−β)−3.03At β = 30°, pco = 5 mm, Dh = 4 mm → Ge₃ ≈ 17,361, Ge₄ ≈ −1.120
Dh 4 mm · β 30° · pco 5 mm · b 2.34 mm (derived) · Lw 200 mm · Lv 0.5 m — from HX geometry
Selected model→Kelvion HP DW 500H · double-wall · ≈84 plates

Condenser — Brazed-Plate HX

Condenser — three-zone model

  • Three zones by enthalpy, not length — 14.0 / 81.5 / 4.5%: one LMTD suffices; subcool gives the EEV 5 K liquid — no receiver.
  • Design point: 182 kW at tc = 45 °C, glycol 38 → 42 °C; U = 2,000 W/m²K → 12.2 m²: ≈104 plates.
ΔP — refrigerant side (condensing zone)ΔPfr = f · (Lv N / Dh) · GEq²/ρf  —  integrated over 200 quality steps, x = 0 → 1 (np.trapezoid)
Desuperheat + subcool zones (Blasius): ΔP = 2f · (G²/ρ) · Lzone/DhN = 2 is Han’s rig constant, not Ncp = 47 (else 23.5× over) · Gc ≈ 25.1 — inside Han’s 13–34 band
ΔP — glycol sideΔP = 2f · (Lv/Dh) · G²/ρ  —  Martin (1996)11.64 kg/s · Re = 2,703 (turbulent branch) · f = 0.107 → ΔP = 35.91 kPa4× the evaporator water loss at equal area — ~2× flow, denser fluid, ΔP ∝ G²
Where f comes from — Han (2003), condensation setGe₃ = 3,521.1·(pco/Dh)+4.17·(π/2−β)−7.75Same β and pco as the evaporator → Ge₃ ≈ 6,246, Ge₄ ≈ −0.985Sign flips (+4.17 vs −5.27): film growth vs nucleation — f ≈ 2.8× lower

Free-Cooling Economizer

Economizer Selection and Modeling

  • Hardest duty point: 100% of 150 kW at 4 °C OAT (ASHRAE 90.1 §6.5.1)
  • LMTD at that point → area → ≈49 plates · single-wall — no refrigerant contact
  • Payoff: 2,542 h/yr of compressor-off free cooling — 29% of the year
ΔP — both sides, same equationΔP = 2f · (Lv/Dh) · G²/ρ,   G = ṁ/(Ncp b Lw)Water: Re = 1,617 · f = 0.105 → 6.67 kPa  |  Glycol: Re = 456 · f = 0.142 → 10.43 kPaBoth on the laminar branch — the condenser’s glycol at Re = 2,703 is turbulent

Compressors

Compressor Selection and Modeling

  • Two parallel 75 kW units on one shared circuit — redundancy + staging
  • Modeled by the manufacturer's AHRI-540 polynomial map — reproduces Bitzer's rating (74.9 kW, 22.74 kW, COP 3.55) exactly
  • VFDs trim full-load speed to ≈75% for exactly 150 kW, and carry the 5:1 turndown without hot-gas bypass
  • Modeling: three coefficient sets — capacity, power, mass flow — each evaluated at (to, tc); solve_with_vfd() finds the speed that delivers the load exactly
y = c₁ + c₂to + c₃tc + c₄to² + c₅totc + c₆tc² + c₇to³ + c₈tcto² + c₉totc² + c₁₀tc³AHRI-540 / EN 12900 — separate coefficient sets for Q̇, P and ṁ
Q̇(N) = (N/N₀) · Q̇₀   ·   P(N) = (N/N₀) · P₀VFD model — capacity and power scale ≈linearly with speed for a recip, so COP is speed-invariant
Selected model→2 × Bitzer 4FEP-35Z · semi-hermetic recip · VFD

Electronic Expansion Valve

Expansion Valve Selection and Modeling

  • One EEV feeds the full 150 kW shared circuit, holding target evaporator superheat
  • Sized by the incompressible-liquid orifice equation: A ≈ 27.1 mm² → d ≈ 5.9 mm (Cd = 0.65)
  • Delivers the spec: 82.6% open at design, 24.3% at 30 kW — controllable across the whole 5:1 band, no low-load hunting from an oversized valve
  • Modeling: calc_orifice() inverts the orifice equation at each load — required area over full area gives the opening fraction tracked across the turndown
ṁr = Cd · Aorifice · √(2 ρin · ΔP)   →   Aorifice = ṁr / (Cd √(2 ρin ΔP))Incompressible-liquid orifice equation, inverted for the required flow area
opening = Areq(load) / Aorifice  →  82.6% at 150 kW · 24.3% at 30 kWValve-authority check across the 5:1 turndown (controls chapter)
Selected model→Danfoss ETS / Emerson EX class · single valve

Oil Separator

Oil Separator Selection and Modeling

  • Returns oil from the common discharge to the crankcases — critical with two parallel compressors sharing one circuit
  • Selected from Bitzer's chart by maximum suction volume flow — staying in the single-vendor Bitzer ecosystem
  • 18 L oil charge; permitted for R-290 per Bitzer DB-300-9 / DP-500-2
  • Modeling: the compressor map’s mass flow and suction density give the worst-case volume flow — no thermal model needed, selection is a chart-band lookup
V̇suction = ṁr / ρsuction  ·  2 units → 203.6 m³/h  →  OA1954Bitzer quick-selection chart: sized on combined suction volume flow

Dry Cooler

Dry Cooler Selection and Modeling

  • Duty: 670 MBH at 42/38 °C glycol · 35 °C air — the 3 K approach that anchors tc = 45 °C and protects COP
  • Modeling: cross-flow ε-NTU — sizing runs it backward (root-find NTU → UA = 73.7 kW/K), off-design runs it forward holding UA
  • No coil geometry from the vendor → ΔP by vendor-anchored scaling: in the Blasius ratio, L, D and circuit count cancel — one rated point (55.2 kPa) scales to any condition (23.2 kPa at free cooling)
  • Variable-speed EC fans (6 × 0.91 m, 60 dB(A)) — cube-law fan power in the annual model
ε = 1 − exp{(1/Cr) NTU0.22 [exp(−Cr NTU0.78) − 1]}  ·  UA = NTU · CminCross-flow ε-NTU (both streams unmixed) — NTU found by numerical root-finding
ΔP₂/ΔP₁ = (ρ₂/ρ₁)0.8 (ṁ₂ρ₁/ṁ₁ρ₂)1.8 (μ₂/μ₁)0.2Vendor-anchored ΔP scaling — geometry cancels; needs one rated point + CoolProp only

Circulation Pumps

Pump Selection and Modeling

  • Glycol loop — 2.5AC: 41.4 m³/h @ 14.2 m (11.64 kg/s of 30% PG), ≈2.57 kW shaft
  • Chilled-water loop — 1.5AD: 7.0 in trim, 1.5 hp, serving the CRAH circuit
  • Digitized for modeling: vendor curve sheets traced per impeller trim (5.0–7.0 in) and least-squares fit — the model runs on the fitted curves, not catalog points
  • Both on VFDs — pump parasitics track flow in the annual PUE model instead of running flat-out
  • Modeling: fitted H(Q) intersected with the loop resistance curve; the digitized efficiency map turns duty into shaft power for the 8,760-h PUE model
H(Q) = c₂Q² + c₁Q + c₀   ·   Pshaft = ρ g Q H / ηpumpQuadratic fit to each digitized trim curve · trim chosen where H(Q) clears the margined duty
Q ∝ N  ·  H ∝ N²  ·  P ∝ N³Affinity laws — VFD part-load power in the annual model
