              International Refrigeration and Compressor Course 2026

   Project #3: Water-Cooled Chiller for High Efficiency Edge Compute Data
                                              Center Facility

During the IRCC your task is designing an air-cooled chiller for a high efficiency edge compute data
center facility near Champaign, IL. The cycle architecture for the chiller is entirely up to you and
must meet the capacity and energy efficiency requirements as stated below. Details regarding the
load and environmental conditions are given below.

Subtasks:
     1. Cycle design
                a) Problem specification:
                     · Vapor compression refrigerant: to be chosen (GWP < 150, A1 or A2L/A3 only
                           with charge analysis)
                     · Chilled fluid: water to Computer Room Air Handler (CRAH)/in-row units (data
                           hall side)
                     · Heat rejection: dry cooler with intermediate water/propylene-glycol (PG) loop
                     · Maximum cooling load: 150 kW ( 42 refrigeration tons) sensible-dominated IT

                     · Maximum outdoor temperature: TMY3 weather data; dry-cooler design at 35

Notes on computing each metric:

     · Full-load COP: compute at AHRI 550/590-2023 standard rating conditions (Leaving chilled
          water temperature (LCHW) 6.7 °C, condenser entering air 35 °C). For this study report
          COP at the project-specific design point as well.

     · Integrated part load value (IPLV.IP): weighted average per AHRI 550/590-2023 §5.2 --
          0.01·A + 0.42·B + 0.45·C + 0.12·D where A, B, C, D are COPs at 100/75/50/25 % load with
          reduced condenser entering temperatures (35 / 26.7 / 18.3 / 12.8 °C).

     · Power useage effectiveness (PUE): (IT load + other facility energy loads) / IT load on a
          design-day basis.

     · Economizer: required by ASHRAE 90.1-2022 §6.5.1; the dry-cooler loop must be capable
          of providing 100 % of the design cooling load at the specified outdoor air temperature
          (OAT), via a glycol-to-CHW plate heat exchanger in parallel with the chiller.
